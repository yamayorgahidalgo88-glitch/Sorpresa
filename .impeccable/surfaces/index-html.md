# Surface brief: home (index.html)

Scope: the barbershop's one-page site plus the AI cut advisor section. Visitor mode: Persuade (advisor block behaves as an Operate tool inside the page).
Audience: young men and families of Alcorcón; job: decide on a cut and book. Action: "Reservar en Booksy" (external, the shop's live booking). Proof: real Booksy listing (5,0, 131 reseñas, real prices). Constraint: no invented reviews, photos, hours.
Build path: code-led (no image generation in this harness).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a dance bill screened on fluorescent card where haircuts are the acts and the ranking is by how well each cut suits your face. Refuses the black-and-gold barber-pole template and the white spa minimal.

OWN-WORLD: Fluo yellow card ground, fluo pink second stock, heavy black ink with slight bleed and screen grain. Outlined block caps (Archivo, expanded black, stroked) for ranked acts; condensed bold caps for labels; black-boxed kickers; hand-ruled 3px black rules and date-grid tables; one pink tape band struck at an angle with stars. Halftone dots are the shading language: a fade is drawn as a halftone ramp.

STORY: Visitor reads the bill (real services ranked, shop name struck across), learns it is Ayoub's barbershop in Alcorcón rated 5,0 on Booksy, then either books on Booksy or screens their own photo onto the poster and watches the bill re-rank into their personal lineup, then tries the top act on.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead box top-left (AYOUB LAHMANY ★ BARBER SHOP · ALCORCÓN), ruled nav top. Center at full width: four outlined ranked lines of services with prices, a pink tape "★ AYOUB LAHMANY ★" struck diagonally across lines 2-3. Under a rule: strapline left, pink boxed RESERVAR EN BOOKSY right, secondary "¿QUÉ CORTE TE VA?" link. Below the fold edge: the hand-ruled price grid begins.

FORM: posters-covers-sleeves-soundsystem-dance-poster, chosen by user over grounded list (assigned #6 Metro line). seed:609899a8a0659ca6. Signature interaction: photo screen-printed as halftone onto the card, ink calipers draw measurements, the bill FLIP-re-ranks; active filter gains a second outline and drop.
